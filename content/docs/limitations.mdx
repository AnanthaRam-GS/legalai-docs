<Callout kind="disclaimer" title="Not legal advice">
  LegalAI is a research prototype built for an academic project. It does not provide legal advice,
  and its output must not be relied on as a substitute for a qualified lawyer. Always check an
  answer against the cited judgment and the current statute, and consult a lawyer before acting on
  it.
</Callout>

## Coverage

- **A small corpus.** Answers draw on 70 reviewed judgments. Many questions will fall outside it; LegalAI is designed to say so rather than answer from general knowledge, but a "no coverage" answer means only that the corpus lacks material, not that no law applies.
- **Criminal law only,** in English, centred on the IPC, BNS, CrPC and BNSS and a few special Acts cited in judgments.
- **The IPC to BNS transition** is thinly covered: BNS and BNSS account for 33 of the 920 section citations extracted from the corpus, and only 3 of the 30 benchmark queries test the transition.

## Answers

- **Grounding is checked structurally.** Citation validation confirms that each claim cites evidence that was actually retrieved, with matching metadata. It does not prove that the cited passage supports the claim in a legal sense.
- **Relative relevance is not a probability.** It ranks precedents within one answer; a score of 1.0 means "best of these five", not "certainly relevant".
- **Predicted sections are signals,** used to guide retrieval, not legal conclusions.
- **OCR can misread scans.** Low-confidence pages are flagged, but names, dates and figures from scans should be checked against the original.
- **Professional answers are not available live** on the current provider tier: the professional answer does not fit the provider's output limits. This is a capacity limit, not a design choice, and it is being worked on.

## Analytics

- Cited sections and outcomes are extracted by rules and are **unreviewed** until a qualified reviewer checks them. Outcome fields are empty when the rules find nothing or find conflicting values.
- The IPC-to-BNS mapping is a comparative reference, not statutory text, and is **unreviewed**; seven pairs printed by NCRB are missing from it.
- NCRB figures are 2024 registrations. They do not describe convictions, acquittals or trends across years.

## Evaluation

- Retrieval quality was measured on **30 queries** labelled by **one reviewer**, using pooled rather than exhaustive relevance, over the same small corpus. The figures are historical and descriptive, not statistically significant.
- Citation validity under the current prompt rests on a **handful of live runs** (5 of 5 citations valid).
- There has been **no user acceptance testing** yet.

## Engineering

- Rate limits and the generation cap live in memory, so they reset on restart and do not span several server processes.
- Logout does not revoke a copied token before it expires (60 minutes).
- The system has not yet been deployed.
